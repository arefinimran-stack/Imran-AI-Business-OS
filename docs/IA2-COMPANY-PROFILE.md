# IA² Holdings — Company Profile (Working Draft)

**Status:** Strategic profile draft only. This document does not represent that every business line is incorporated, licensed, funded, staffed or operational.

## Positioning
IA² Holdings is being developed as a Bangladesh-focused venture development, investment facilitation and business intelligence platform. It connects international commercial demand, local execution capacity, investment opportunities and practical technology solutions.

## Mission
Turn verified market intelligence and real operational problems into commercially viable ventures and measurable solutions, while connecting international businesses with trusted Bangladesh-based legal, corporate and operational support.

## Business lines

### 1. Bangladesh Market Entry & Investment Facilitation
- Market-entry research and feasibility assessments.
- Company, branch, liaison or representative-office setup coordination where legally appropriate.
- Joint-venture partner identification and preliminary due diligence.
- FDI project preparation and facilitation through relevant authorities.
- Local representation under written mandates.
- Regulatory, tax, licensing and compliance coordination with qualified advisers.

### 2. International Garment Buyer & Sourcing Desk
- Identify overseas brands, retailers, importers, wholesalers and sourcing companies with evidence of Bangladesh sourcing or expansion intent.
- Qualify product, volume, target price, quality, certification, delivery and destination-market requirements.
- Match buyer requirements with independently verified Bangladesh manufacturers/exporters.
- Coordinate samples, quotations, factory visits, audits and order milestones through accountable local partners.
- Manage buyer, supplier, order and follow-up records in the IA² CRM.

IA² must not describe itself as a garment manufacturer, exporter, buying house or authorised agent unless the relevant entity, partner agreements, registrations and authority are established and verified.

### 3. AI Intelligence & Public Problem-Solving
- Research government and institutional service-delivery bottlenecks.
- Develop evidence-based problem statements and solution concepts.
- Build responsible AI and workflow-automation prototypes.
- Prepare measurable pilot proposals, cost-benefit cases, implementation plans and procurement-ready documentation.
- Measure processing time, cost per case, backlog, error rate, access and user satisfaction.

### 4. Venture Development & Investment Readiness
- Discover and screen business opportunities.
- Validate demand, competition, unit economics and execution capacity.
- Build financial models, scenarios, cash-flow forecasts and investment memoranda.
- Prepare data rooms, pitch materials and due-diligence responses.
- Introduce qualified founders, operators, investors and strategic partners.

Regulatory boundary: this profile does not claim that IA² operates a licensed venture-capital fund, manages third-party investment assets, provides regulated securities advice or solicits public investment. Those activities require legal and regulatory review, appropriate structuring, permissions and disclosures.

### 5. Real Estate & Housing — planned vertical
Market and feasibility research, project and land due-diligence coordination, development-partner identification, housing technology opportunities and transaction support through qualified professionals. No brokerage, development, valuation or investment-management capability should be advertised as active until credentials, mandates, partners and permissions are confirmed.

### 6. Portfolio ventures — research / hold
- C3 Lifestyle: market research and opportunity discovery only; execution on hold.
- Bold Cotton: product and buyer-market research only; execution on hold.

## Legal execution lead
Arefin Legal & Corporate Group is intended to lead legal and corporate execution, including Bangladesh company formation, corporate structuring, MOA/AOA, shareholders' and JV agreements, partnership and commercial agreements, FDI and market-entry legal coordination, regulatory issue-spotting, due diligence and transaction documentation.

Legal services must be separately scoped, conflict-checked, engagement-letter governed and billed. Legal fees must remain distinct from commercial introduction or sourcing fees, subject to professional conduct rules.

## Target customers
1. International garment buyers and sourcing companies seeking Bangladesh supply.
2. Foreign manufacturers and investors evaluating Bangladesh operations or joint ventures.
3. Overseas SMEs and mid-market companies requiring local market-entry support.
4. Local businesses seeking international buyers, partners, capital or export access.
5. Public institutions and development partners seeking measurable AI-enabled service improvements.
6. Investors and strategic partners considering evidence-backed Bangladesh opportunities.

## Revenue model hypotheses
Fixed-fee market-entry and feasibility assignments; legal retainers and matter-based fees; corporate formation and transaction documentation; sourcing coordination under written mandates; local representation retainers; commissioned research; technology discovery and pilot contracts; venture project-development fees subject to regulatory review.

No success fee, commission, referral fee or investor-introduction fee should be charged where prohibited by law, professional rules or contract.

## Public-facing description — proposed
IA² Holdings is a Bangladesh-focused venture development and business facilitation platform connecting international companies with local legal, corporate, sourcing and execution support. Through evidence-led market intelligence and its AI-enabled operating platform, IA² identifies commercial opportunities, supports market entry and joint-venture preparation, and develops practical technology solutions to institutional and public-service challenges.

## Governance and credibility gate
Before external circulation, approve and verify the exact registered entity name, registration number, ownership, registered address, directors, authorised signatories, group-company relationships, professional credentials, team, offices, partner network, certifications, factory access, client references, financial figures, licences and regulatory permissions.

Never invent clients, buyers, factory audits, certifications, government endorsements, project wins, revenue, investment commitments or operating capacity.
