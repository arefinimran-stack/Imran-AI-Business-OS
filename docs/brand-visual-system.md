# IMRAN AI — Brand Visual System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Editorial design standard  
**Applies to:** Arefin Legal & Corporate, Imran Arefin, Arefin Avenue, IA² Holdings, C3 Lifestyle and Bold Cotton.

## 1. Design principle

The ecosystem should feel premium, contemporary, culturally aware and recognisable. Premium does not mean using black and gold everywhere. Use a deliberate mix of expressive colour, editorial typography, excellent imagery, generous spacing and consistent composition.

Pinterest is a trend-discovery and moodboard reference, not a source to copy. Review its current colour forecasts and visual search patterns periodically. The 2026 Pinterest Palette identifies Cool Blue (#D7EFFF), Jade (#AEB8A0), Plum Noir (#351E28), Wasabi (#E9F056) and Persimmon (#FF5C34). Pinterest also highlights Cool-Wasabi and Persimmon-Plum as pairings. - Accent: Champagne; Cool Blue for explainers and data-led slides.

- Primary: Plum Noir, Warm Ivory, Ink.
- Accent: Champagne, Jade or Persimmon in campaign-specific work.

## 4. Composition rules

1. Use one dominant colour, one supporting colour, one accent and a neutral. Do not give every colour equal visual weight.
2. Keep high-saturation Wasabi and Persimmon as accents unless the campaign specifically calls for a bold full-field treatment.
3. Maintain readable text contrast, especially on mobile. Never place small text on busy photography.
4. Use a consistent type hierarchy: headline, subhead, body, source/caption. Prefer restrained type families and avoid mixing too many fonts.
5. Use real, licensed or permissioned photography and accurately represent people, products and places.
6. Keep logo clear space, consistent margins and platform-safe areas.
7. Design for the destination format: vertical video, square/portrait feed, carousel/document, YouTube thumbnail and LinkedIn document each need their own composition.
8. Do not use gradients, metallic effects, shadows or decorative flourishes as substitutes for strong layout and imagery.
9. Ensure charts and infographics are legible, sourced and numerically faithful.
10. Treat AI-generated imagery as illustrative. Never present generated people, places, product samples or documentary scenes as real evidence.

## 5. Creative workflow

For each campaign:
1. Define audience, message, content genre and desired action.
2. Select a brand palette and one campaign accent.
3. Build a moodboard using Pinterest visual research, photography references and typography/layout references.
4. Create original compositions; record reference links and asset licences.
5. Check accessibility, brand fit, factual accuracy and platform cropping.
6. Store the final design source, exported assets, palette tokens and version in the content record or approved asset library.

## 6. Review checklist

- [ ] Correct brand identity and palette
- [ ] Original composition; references are inspiration, not copied artwork
- [ ] Premium visual hierarchy and mobile legibility
- [ ] Real/accurate imagery and cleared usage rights
- [ ] Claims, statistics and labels checked against sources
- [ ] Contrast and accessibility checked
- [ ] Platform-specific dimensions and safe areas checked
- [ ] Final approved asset and source file archived

## Founder identity, portrait and contact integrity

All founder-led, advocate-led, author-led and presenter-led visual materials must use the identity of Imran Arefin when a named individual is required. Use the founder's approved original reference photograph as the facial identity source. Creative treatments may change clothing, background, lighting, pose framing, crop, colour grade, illustration style and production setting, but must preserve the founder's actual face and recognisable facial features. Do not substitute, blend, fabricate or use another person's face as Imran Arefin.

When a design identifies the advocate, founder, author or presenter by name, use the name **Imran Arefin**. Use only the founder's verified professional email address and contact number supplied directly by the founder in contact blocks. Never invent, borrow or substitute another person's contact details. Do not expose personal contact details in source code, public repositories, metadata or internal documents unless a specific public-facing placement has been authorised. For any design requiring a portrait but lacking access to the approved reference image, hold the portrait element rather than generating an unrelated face.
