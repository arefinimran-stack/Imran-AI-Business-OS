# IA² Buyer Intelligence — Japan Apparel Prospect Batch 01

**Research date:** 15 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Build an initial, evidence-backed prospect batch for Bangladesh apparel sourcing and market-entry services.  
**Qualification status:** These are verified companies with relevant business activity and documented Bangladesh-market signals. They are not confirmed live RFQs, purchase orders or authorised procurement contacts. Keep them at Grade B until the actual requirement and decision-maker are confirmed.

## Why Japan first

- Bangladesh RMG exports to Japan reached USD 343.65 million in July–September FY2026–27, 3.25% of Bangladesh's total apparel exports in that quarter. The reported Japanese apparel import market reached USD 24.19 billion in FY2025–26. Source: BSS / EPB / ITC reporting. (https://www.bssnews.net/business/438330)
- Japanese buyers expressed interest in increasing imports of high-value and fashionable apparel from Bangladesh at a BGMEA meeting with a Japan Textile Importers Association delegation. Named companies included MN Inter-Fashion, Kowa Company, Toyoshima & Co. and AIT Corporation. (https://unb.com.bd/category/Business/japanese-buyers-keen-to-source-high-value-fashionable-apparel-from-bangladesh/172772)
- BGMEA and Marubeni agreed to a roadmap for expanding Japanese apparel exports, including Marubeni sharing three-year sourcing plans and BGMEA curating SME factories for Japanese demand. (https://www.bgmea.com.bd/page/BGMEA_and_Marubeni_Corporation_Forge_Strategic_Partnership_to_Boost_Apparel_Exports_to_Japan)
- BGMEA launched a Japan Desk and selected approximately 100 factories for work with Japanese buyers; senior representatives of Teijin Frontier, Marubeni, ITOCHU and Toyota Tsusho attended. (https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/rmg/bgmea-launches-japan-desk-tap-bigger-share-japans-22b-apparel-market-1552016)

## Immediate commercial approach

1. Prepare a one-page IA² Japan market-entry and sourcing capability brief.
2. Prepare a buyer-specific note for each company; do not send generic bulk outreach.
3. Ask BGMEA, JBCCI, JETRO Dhaka and the relevant trade associations for formal introduction channels.
4. Identify the correct business unit and procurement owner through official company channels.
5. Offer a paid Bangladesh sourcing / supplier-readiness assessment before promising factory matching.
6. Do not claim access to selected BGMEA factories, exclusivity, agency or an existing buyer relationship unless documented.

## First offer to these prospects

**Bangladesh Apparel Sourcing & Market-Entry Brief**
- Category-specific supplier landscape and capacity review.
- Factory compliance and certification requirements.
- Product / quality / MOQ / lead-time questionnaire.
- Bangladesh market-entry and local operating options.
- Indicative risk register and next-step plan.
- Legal and corporate setup scope, separately engaged through Arefin Legal & Corporate Group.

## Verification limitations

The cited sources establish that the companies exist, have relevant business activity and were involved in public Bangladesh/Japan apparel discussions. They do not establish that any company has an open RFQ today, has authorised IA² to represent it, or will award an order. Confirm buyer authority, current requirement, procurement channel and commercial terms before grading any prospect A or forecasting revenue.
