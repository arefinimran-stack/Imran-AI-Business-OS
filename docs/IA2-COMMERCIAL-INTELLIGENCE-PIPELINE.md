# IA² Commercial Intelligence & International Buyer Pipeline

**Execution priority:** verified commercial enquiries and paid assignments.  
**Deferred ventures:** C3 Lifestyle and Bold Cotton remain on research-only hold.  
**Primary geography:** international companies seeking to source from, establish in or expand within Bangladesh.

## 1. First revenue thesis
The shortest credible route to initial revenue is a Bangladesh Market Entry & Garment Buyer Desk:
1. Identify a real overseas buyer, importer, retailer, brand or sourcing company.
2. Verify the legal entity, active business, buying category and official contact route.
3. Establish evidence of Bangladesh sourcing, supplier search or expansion intent.
4. Record source evidence and confidence in CRM.
5. Qualify the commercial requirement.
6. Offer a scoped paid service: sourcing brief, supplier shortlist, factory due-diligence coordination, market-entry assessment or legal/corporate setup consultation.
7. Convert the engagement into a signed mandate and track delivery, fee and collection.

A public website proves existence only. It does not prove buying authority, current purchase intent, creditworthiness or a genuine requirement.

## 2. Priority customer segments
### International garment buyers
Apparel brands and retailers; importers and wholesalers; private-label, uniform and workwear buyers; e-commerce apparel brands with repeat purchasing; sourcing offices; companies announcing supplier diversification or Bangladesh expansion.

### Bangladesh market entrants
Overseas garment manufacturers and textile groups; brands seeking local sourcing or production offices; investors evaluating manufacturing, logistics or textiles; SMEs seeking local representation or JV partners.

### Import/export product research
Knit and woven apparel, denim, sportswear, workwear and uniforms; man-made fibre and technical textiles; apparel accessories and packaging; home textiles; jute products; leather goods and footwear; light engineering and selected industrial inputs. Pharmaceuticals and chemicals require additional regulatory and quality screening.

## 3. Geographic sequencing
Initial research priority: Japan; United States; United Kingdom and European Union; Australia and Canada; South Korea, UAE, Saudi Arabia and other emerging markets. This is a research order, not a claim that any named buyer is currently seeking suppliers.

## 4. Lead verification standard
Capture legal company name, trading name, country, registered address, official website, category, product lines, named contact and role where public, official business contact channel, active-trading evidence, Bangladesh-intent evidence, source URL, publisher, publication and retrieval dates, provenance, outreach basis, lead grade, estimated deal type/value, risks and next action.

Grades:
- A — verified buyer intent: credible primary evidence of active Bangladesh sourcing/supplier search/expansion plus identifiable company and contact route.
- B — verified company and buyer fit, but current Bangladesh intent not confirmed.
- C — discovery lead from directory, event or secondary source; independent validation required.
- Rejected — unverifiable, suspicious, inconsistent or no legitimate business rationale.

Never grade A based solely on a website, directory listing, email address or AI inference.

## 5. Buyer qualification
Establish product, existing sourcing countries, Bangladesh intent, supplier/office/JV requirement, realistic trial and annual volumes, target FOB/landed price, required audits and certifications, sample and delivery windows, payment terms, procurement decision-maker, and next agreed action/date.

## 6. CRM lifecycle
Discovered → Identity verified → Buyer fit assessed → Bangladesh intent verified → Contact identified → Outreach drafted → Founder approved → Contacted → Replied → Qualified → Meeting → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost.

A prospect cannot become qualified without a documented requirement. Won requires a signed contract, purchase order or other verifiable commitment. Revenue requires actual accounting evidence.

## 7. Outreach controls
Use official business channels and role-relevant contacts. Store source and date for contact details. Respect privacy, marketing, anti-spam laws and platform terms. Do not scrape restricted personal data or use lists of uncertain provenance. No automated WhatsApp/email before founder approval and a verified lawful contact basis. Maintain do-not-contact records and honour opt-outs. Do not imply IA² is a manufacturer, exclusive sourcing agent, government representative or authorised local agent without documentary authority.

2. Verified Supplier Shortlist — matched factory candidates, with documentary checks and limitations stated.
3. Bangladesh Market Entry Assessment — entity options, regulatory map, indicative timeline, costs and workstreams.

5. Local Representation Retainer — only after due diligence, conflict checks, authority, service levels and written mandate.
6. AI / Public-Service Pilot Discovery — paid discovery/feasibility where an institution or sponsor has a defined problem and lawful procurement route.

## 9. Import/export product scoring
Score each product-market pair on import demand and multi-year growth; reachable buyers; Bangladesh supplier capacity; quality and certification readiness; tariffs, rules of origin, trade remedies and logistics; working capital and payment risk; landed cost and gross margin; competition; regulatory/sanctions exposure; and time to first transaction.

High growth alone is insufficient. Every thesis requires HS code, trade data, buyer validation, supplier capacity, compliance review and landed-cost calculation.

## 10. Government AI opportunity pipeline
Define service and institution; collect primary evidence such as official process maps, reports, service statistics, audit findings, citizen feedback and tenders; quantify cost, time, backlog and error; identify legal authority, data sensitivity and procurement route; design a limited measurable pilot with human oversight; build cost-benefit, implementation, security and evaluation plans; seek authorised discovery, grant, innovation challenge, pilot or procurement; report independently measured outcomes.

No government funding, contract, endorsement or access is secured until confirmed in writing.
