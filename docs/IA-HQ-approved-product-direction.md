# IA HQ — Approved Product Direction

**Status:** Approved direction / implementation baseline  
**Brand order:** THE ADVISOR first; IA HQ second.

## Public-facing identity

- **Primary public brand:** THE ADVISOR
- **Public attribution:** LED BY IMRAN AREFIN ONLY
- **Primary commercial focus:** Legal & Corporate services, with emphasis on Bangladesh legal capability and foreign investment / international business advisory.
- Do not publish internal management structures, private operating arrangements, or confidential governance material.

## Central operating platform

**IA HQ** is the unified command centre supporting the approved business portfolio. It is an internal operating platform, not a competing public-facing brand.

- IA² Venture Capital

C3 Lifestyle and Bold Cotton remain in research and opportunity monitoring mode until execution is separately approved.

## Shared Studio principle

Research, writing, design, image, video, CRM, finance, analytics, security, and automation capabilities should be reusable across the portfolio. Prefer shared infrastructure and configurable workflows over duplicated applications or subscriptions.

## Visual system

- Wine Red: `#7F011F`
- Light Sand: `#F5EBD0`
- Direction: premium, lively, clear, editorial, and contemporary.
- Use dimensional iconography and restrained glossy treatments without compromising legibility, accessibility, or responsive behavior.
- Maintain consistent typography, spacing, interaction states, and brand attribution across surfaces.

## Delivery sequence

1. Make THE ADVISOR's core professional website and lead-capture journey reliable.
2. Stabilize IA HQ as the founder's operating interface: navigation, task/project registers, evidence-based status, integration health, and responsive behavior.
3. Establish shared Studio capabilities that can be reused by the portfolio.
4. Validate commercially viable digital services and products before scaling.
5. Keep C3 Lifestyle and Bold Cotton in research mode until approved for execution.

## Engineering and reporting rules

- Preserve existing app identities and working integrations; avoid duplicate apps.
- Treat Notion as the authoritative task/project register where its connection is verified. Clearly identify any fallback or access failure.
- Treat GitHub as the version-control record and AppDeploy as the deployment environment.
- Report work as **completed and verified**, **in progress**, **planned**, or **blocked**. Never label an unverified action as complete.
- Do not expose secrets, API tokens, private governance information, or internal operating arrangements in public files.
- A feature is complete only after its user-visible workflow has been tested and its deployed state has been verified.
