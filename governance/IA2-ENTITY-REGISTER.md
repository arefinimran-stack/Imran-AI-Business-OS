## Register rules

- This is a brand / project inventory, not evidence of incorporation or registration.
- Add every new entity before creating public-facing assets.
- Record legal form, jurisdiction, owners, operating status, public channels, domain, asset folder, document pack, approvals and review dates.
- Separate parent identity from sub-brand identity. Shared design language does not make distinct brands interchangeable.
- No domain or trademark availability is implied by inclusion in this register.
